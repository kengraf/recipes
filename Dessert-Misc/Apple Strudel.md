# Apple Strudel

**Makes** 2 Strudels (4 servings each)  
**Source:** [Step by step, with pics for first timers](https://www.thecountrycook.net/apple-strudel/)

###  Ingredients

  * 1 egg
  * 2 teaspoons water
  * 2 large granny smith apples
  * ½ cup granulated sugar
  * ½ cup golden raisins (optional, can use regular raisins or dried cranberries)
  * ⅓ cup panko breadcrumbs (can use regular)
  * 2 teaspoons fresh squeezed lemon juice
  * ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon
  * ⅛ teaspoon salt
  * 17.3 ounce box frozen puff pastry, thawed (2 in a box)
  * 2-3 Tablespoons powdered sugar (optional)

###  Directions

* Preheat oven to 400 degrees
* line a large, rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper.
* In a small bowl, whisk together 1 egg and 2 teaspoons water, set aside.
* Peel and core 2 large granny smith apples, slice into ¼ inch; ½ apple slices. 
* In a large bowl add the sliced apples and the remaining igredients, gently toss until combined.
* Line work surface with a 14×16-inch piece of parchment paper, lightly dust with flour.
* Roll a puff pastry sheet into a 12×14-inch rectangle on the parchment paper.
* Place half of the apple filling onto the puff pastry, between the middle and bottom third of the puff pastry,leaving a 2-inch border on each side, and a 3-inch border on the bottom.
* Using the parchment paper, lift the bottom piece of pastry over the apple filling. Fold the outer edges of the pastry toward the center. Using the parchment paper, continue to roll the strudel, ensuring the outer edges are tucked in to prevent any leaking.
* Place strudel on the prepared baking sheet seam side down, form the second strudel just like the first one.
* Brush both strudels with the prepared egg wash.
* Using a sharp knife cut 3-4 (1-inch) slits in the top each strudel to allow steam to escape.
* Bake both strudels for 25 minutes or until a deep golden brown. 
* Transfer strudels to a wire rack to cool at least 20 minutes.
* Before serving, dust with 2-3 Tablespoons powdered sugar.
* Using a serrated knife cut strudel into desired serving sizes.
